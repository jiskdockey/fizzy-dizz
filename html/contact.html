I will adjust the currency on the invoice to fit the one YOU will be paying with, so don't worry about conversion fees.


You may choose to split your payment only if the price is over £40/$50, in which case you can pay 50% upfront and the remaining 50% after the the initial sketch is finalised.


You may only request changes (changing poses, expressions, details, etc.) during the initial sketch phase. I cannot allow anymore revisions after this, unless I personally make a mistake.


It is expected that you will not request a refund once payment has been received.

However, if I haven't yet started your commission, or decide to cancel it for any reason, I will issue a 100% refund. If I'm already at a certain stage in progress, a partial refund will be given depending on where I am.
